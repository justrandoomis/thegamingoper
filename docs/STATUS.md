## 3. Open items

1. ~~**Hydration warning on every page**~~ — fixed 2026-11-11 (nonce on `<Links>`, `<Scripts>`, `<ScrollRestoration>`). Was: `<Links />` renders the critical-CSS `<link>` with the request nonce on the server but `nonce={undefined}` on the client (the client's framework context has no nonce). Fix: `<Links nonce={data?.nonce} />` (same for `<Scripts>` and `<ScrollRestoration>`); React 19.3 compares `node.nonce`, so hidden nonces still match.
2. ~~**Brand files not in the head**~~ — done 2026-11-11. Was: favicon `.ico` + `.svg`, `apple-touch-icon`, `site.webmanifest`, `theme-color` (light `#F3EFEC`, dark from tokens).
3. ~~**No scheduled jobs**~~ — done 2026-11-11 (section 1, Scheduled jobs). Follow-ups: prune `job_runs`; copy the daily `audit_seal` log lines somewhere durable. Was: `workers/app.ts` has no `scheduled` handler and `wrangler.jsonc` no cron: `sealAudit` (log its `headHash` outside the database), `cleanupRateLimits`, reservation expiry, and retention of `auth_events`, `otp_codes`, expired sessions and the outbox.
4. **No SMS/WhatsApp/email provider.** Codes and messages are held; phone numbers are unverified; no self-service password reset.
5. **No malware scanning** of uploads (platform limit); defence is the narrow type list and safe serving.
6. **No admin sign-in or setup pages** (`/admin/login`, `/admin/setup` 404); the first owner cannot be created from a browser yet. The local demo employee and customer of ARCHITECTURE §11 (`.dev.seed-credentials`) are created by nobody.
7. **Cloudflare edge settings** (SECURITY.md §13) are unrecorded; HSTS and SPF/DMARC await the owner.
8. **Remote D1:** see the table; the seed SQL file has never been applied remotely.
9. ~~**Catalogue import.**~~ — done 2026-11-11: the demo seed imports the 105 researched products with their photos (section 1, Demo catalogue) and the clay pipeline, `three` and the `art` script are deleted; `npm run catalog:import` (= `db:seed -- --overwrite`) refreshes descriptive content from the catalogue files. Was: the demo seed had its own 51 products with clay keys.
10. **Logo:** provisional trace; the client should supply the original file, approve or reject the `full` lockup, and confirm the brand red.
11. **Demo data everywhere:** prices, stock and the USD rate are placeholders; `STORE_MODE=demo` keeps `noindex` and the demo flag on.
12. **Security follow-ups** (SECURITY.md §14): PBKDF2 capped at 100,000 iterations by the platform; staff two-step verification optional; no `DATA_KEY` re-encryption tool; `auth_events.identifier` stores full phone numbers.
13. **Weak product images:** Seagate expansion card hero (271×501; seagate.com blocks automated requests), Xbox Wireless Headset images 2–3 and the Steam Wallet card are small; a few dark or grey tiles (Razer BlackShark, Game Pass, Series X Digital angle); PS5 and Pulse 3D heroes stay opaque (white product on white). Better official sources wanted.
14. **Append-only tables vs `INSERT OR REPLACE`** (data layer): add BEFORE INSERT triggers that refuse an existing id or idempotency key on ledgers and history tables, update `creditWallet` and `sealAudit` (they rely on duplicate-key errors), then drop `.fails` in `tests/commerce-adversarial.test.ts`.
15. **Store warranty policy (client):** `products.warranty_months` is empty on purpose — delivery opens a warranty record the store must honour, so it needs the store's own terms, not the maker's (those stay in the specs). Also open from the import: English typos ("playstaion") find nothing and "xbox series x" ranks the Series S first (search service), and digital codes cost 92–96 % of their placeholder price because codes sell at face value.
